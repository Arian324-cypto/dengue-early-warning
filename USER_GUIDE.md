# How to use the dashboard

1. Open the sidebar. Choose a week (default shows the riskiest week of 2025), a city and a ward.
2. The big card shows the risk level: Low, Medium, High or Critical.
3. "In plain words" explains the forecast and a suggested action.
4. The bar chart shows why: red bars push risk up.
5. The map shows every ward at once. Use the priority list to see which to target first.
